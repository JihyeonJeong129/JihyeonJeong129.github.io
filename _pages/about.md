Hi! I'm Jihyeon Jeong (정지현), a passionate undergraduate student at Ajou University, double-majoring in Software and Electrical Engineering.

With a deep curiosity in systems, hardware, and AI, I’ve built my own home lab where I run experimental nodes for FPGA, GPU computing, backend services, and automation projects. My interests range from embedded systems and distributed infrastructure to data analysis and computer vision.

I served as an active-duty airman at the 1st Master Control and Reporting Center (1MCRC), stationed at Osan Air Base, where I gained hands-on experience in military-grade systems and high-responsibility communication environments.

I'm always eager to learn, build, and collaborate on challenging technical problems. Thanks for visiting my site!
